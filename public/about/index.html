Gymmy

A workout log and rep timer that lives on your phone — and only on your phone.

Open the app View the source

Privacy first — literally no server

Gymmy is a web app you install from your browser. After the first load it runs entirely on your device: your plan, every logged set, every note is stored in the phone's local storage. There is no account, no cloud, no cookies, and no server that could see your data — the hosting only hands your phone the app code. If you're offline in a basement gym, everything still works.

What is counted, and only this: to know whether anyone uses Gymmy, visits to this page are counted, and the app sends one anonymous “opened today / this week / this month” signal plus a single “first use” signal. They go to GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter without cookies. They carry no ID and none of your training data. Like any website visit, GoatCounter also notes the browser, system and country with each count, but not your IP address, and counts are never linked to each other. You can switch the app's count off in Plan → Settings.

The flip side, stated honestly: your data exists in exactly one place. The Backup tab exports everything to a single file you keep wherever you choose (Files, iCloud, AirDrop) — backing up is your job, and the app reminds you every few sessions.

What it does

📋 A plan that teaches you

Workouts with target sets × reps, weights and rest — plus per-exercise technique: how to do it step by step, common faults to watch for, and when to progress. One tap mid-set shows the form cues.

✍️ One-tap logging

Sets are prefilled from last time, so repeating is a single tick. Or type/dictate "goblet 16 3x8 felt easy" and it's parsed into sets.

📈 Progression that decides for you

Double progression built in: hit the top of the rep range → it suggests more weight next time; miss the bottom twice → it suggests backing off. No spreadsheet math between sets.

⏱ Timers

A rest countdown after every ticked set (with beeps and a voice "Go"), and a hold/swap rep timer with voice cues for timed exercises — screen stays awake through the workout.

🤖 Works with any AI — on your terms

No AI is wired in. Instead, you copy: a prompt teaches Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini the app's format; paste your plan or dictate your session to the AI, paste its answer back. You decide what leaves your phone, every single time.

Honest limitations

  • On iPhone, a backgrounded web app is frozen: if you switch to another app during a rest, the "Go" sound can't play at zero. On return the app shows the true time and announces how late you are.
  • Data lives on one phone. Lose the phone without a backup file, lose the log. (Make backups.)
  • It's a web app: install goes through the browser's "Add to Home Screen", not an app store.

Install it

  • iPhone (Safari): open the app → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): open the app → ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
